# 🐰 Usagi Float

A cute, transparent, always-on-top cross-platform desktop companion for macOS and Windows. Usagi swims across your screen, cheers you up with authentic voice lines ("Una!", "Bryy!", "Huhhhh?"), reminds you to drink water, and helps you study with a built-in Pomodoro timer!

---

## ✨ Features

- **Smooth 60 FPS Swimming**: Kick-and-glide swimming physics with natural turns and gentle screen boundary bounces.
- **Synced Voice Audio**: Authentic sound effects with synchronized speech bubbles (`una.mp3`, `bryy.mp3`, `huhhhh?.mp3`, `urararusk.mp3`, `unayahayahauna.mp3`, `wuuulaaaa.mp3`).
- **Interactive Spin & Combo Spin**: Click Usagi to make him spin faster; click repeatedly (3+ combo) for a super-spin tornado!
- **💧 Gentle Water Reminder**: Randomly every 30–40 minutes, Usagi gently asks you to take a sip of water without locking your screen.
- **😴 AFK Sleep Mode**: Detects when you step away from your desk for > 2 minutes—Usagi takes a nap (`"Zzz..."`) and celebrates with a snack when you come back.
- **🍅 25-Min Pomodoro Timer**: System tray study timer that drops a pudding snack for Usagi to chase down when complete.
- **👻 Smart Ghost Mode (Click-Through)**: Toggle via tray or shortcut (`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+G`) so clicks pass right through to your code/browser.
- **🪄 Play Mode**: Have Usagi follow your mouse cursor across the screen.
